Trust & safety

Strangers, sure. Unknown, never.

ID and phone verification

Every member confirms their identity and phone number before they can post a route or offer a seat. Verified badges show up on every listing.

Two-way ratings, every ride

Drivers rate hitchers, hitchers rate drivers — on punctuality, company, and how safe the ride felt. Low scores surface before anyone gets in the car.

Share your live route

Hitchers can share trip progress with a friend or family member for the duration of the ride, with one tap.

In-app chat before you meet

Confirm pickup details over in-app messages first — no phone numbers exchanged until both sides are comfortable.
