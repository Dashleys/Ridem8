1. Who we are

ridem8 ("we", "us", "the platform") is operated by an individual sole trader based in New Zealand. ridem8 is a website and service (accessible at ridem8.kiwi) that connects drivers with spare seats in their vehicle ("Drivers") with people looking for a ride ("Hitchers") travelling the same route, for the purpose of sharing travel costs.

Note: ridem8 is currently operated by an individual as a sole trader, not a registered company. As a sole trader, there is no legal separation between the operator personally and the business — meaning personal liability is not limited the way it would be under a registered company structure. Before ridem8 scales to real users and real transaction volume, the operator should discuss with an accountant or lawyer whether incorporating as a limited company is advisable, given the platform handles third-party payments and carries transport-related liability exposure. New Zealand's Fair Trading Act 1986 does not appear to impose a blanket requirement for sole traders to publicly disclose their personal legal name on a website; it focuses on accuracy and preventing misleading conduct rather than mandating name disclosure for a business of this kind. The operator's legal name is recorded for tax, banking, and payment-processing purposes as required, consistent with standard sole trader practice. This should still be confirmed with a lawyer as part of a full review of these Terms.

2. What ridem8 is — and isn't

ridem8 is a facilitation platform. We connect Drivers and Hitchers and process payments between them. We are not:

• A taxi, shuttle, or commercial passenger transport service
• A party to any ride arrangement between a Driver and a Hitcher
• An employer of any Driver
• Responsible for the conduct, safety, driving ability, or vehicle condition of any Driver

Rides arranged through ridem8 are intended as cost-sharing arrangements, not commercial passenger transport. Drivers may only recover a reasonable contribution toward the actual costs of the trip (such as fuel, and a fair share of vehicle running costs) — not a profit. It is the Driver's responsibility to ensure prices set through the platform reflect genuine cost-sharing and comply with the Land Transport Rule: Operator Licensing 2017 and any other applicable New Zealand transport law. ridem8 may update pricing guidance or impose pricing limits from time to time to help support compliance, but ultimate responsibility for lawful pricing rests with the Driver.
